# Features

What the Multi-Agent Systems exam preparation platform does.
For setup and day-to-day usage, see [USAGE.md](USAGE.md).

---

## 1. Lessons

Each of the 53 lessons follows the same path:

1. **Understand the Theory**: core idea, detailed explanation, key points.
2. **Visualize the Idea**: what the slide shows, with a button to open it beside the lesson.
3. **Interactive Learning Laboratory**: an embedded lab, opened on the right preset for the lesson.
4. **Step-by-Step Worked Example**: numbered steps with KaTeX math.
5. **Check Your Understanding**: checkpoints with hints and explanations.
6. **Formulas**, exam pitfalls, and personal notes.

Every lesson also has a progress rail, a mastery toggle, a 1–5 confidence rating, bookmarks and a cheat sheet.

## 5. ⚡ Urgent Study, mock exam, motivation

- **Urgent Study**: 15/30/60-minute flashcard sprints from concepts, formulas, definitions and questions, with an exam countdown and weak-card drills.
- **Mock exam**: by chapter or full, timed or not, graded out of 20 with French honours, per-chapter analytics and retake-missed.
- **Agent Society**: XP, 10 levels (Lone Agent → KBE Logician), streaks, achievements and module bosses on a map of all lessons.

## 6. Reference views

Exam Sheet (recto/verso), Cheat Sheets, Formula Sheet (23 formulas), Glossary (65 terms), Comparison Matrices (architectures, organizations, voting rules, Nash vs maximin, S5 vs KD45, STIT vs BIAT, manipulation vs coercion vs persuasion), 241-slide traceability matrix, and global search (`Ctrl/⌘ + K`).
